## 🧠 Using Reading Maps

The core feature of this tool is the sentence map. On the right side of the window, you see a visual guide of the document. Each box represents a block of related ideas. 

*   Click a box to jump to that section in the main text.
*   Use the filter slider to show only the most important sentences.
*   Hover over any section to see a summary of the underlying packet of data.

This map removes the need to scroll through thousands of lines of text. You navigate by concept rather than page number.

## 💾 Saving Your Work

Julia Reader saves your progress automatically. If you close the app, your maps and chunks remain inside the project folder. You do not need to export files unless you want to share them. 

To save a summary for later, go to File > Export as Markdown. This creates a clean text file containing your notes and the maps you created. You can open this file in any text editor like Notepad or Word.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

This software keeps your reading habits local. It does not send your documents to a company server. The intelligent assistant processes your text in small chunks and discards them once the session ends. Your data remains on your machine at all times.
